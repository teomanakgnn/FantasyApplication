How this is worked out. Category strength comes from ESPN's {{ season }} per-game projections, turned into z-scores across the players your league actually drafts. Percentages are weighted by volume. Who is left at each pick comes from ESPN's live average draft position; a player's draft spot is treated as spread around his ADP, tight early and a round wide later. A player is only planned at a pick he is more likely than not to reach, and each pick weighs taking him now against what will still be there at your next one.

How injuries are priced. The projection carries an expected games total; a player flagged today takes a further cut. A missed game costs the gap between the player and a replacement-level one, never more.

What it does not know. Weekly variance, role changes, waiver moves and what the other managers actually do. Treat the category counts as a comparison between strategies, not a forecast.
