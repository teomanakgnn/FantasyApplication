Where the lines come from. These are the Las Vegas season win totals with the price on each side (American odds: -115 means risking 115 to win 100). When the two prices differ, the book expects one side more than the other; "Vegas leans" is that expectation with the bookmaker's margin taken out. Every line ends in .5, so there are no ties.

Our model. Last season's wins pulled toward .500, plus roster strength from each team's best eight players by projected production. Where it disagrees with Vegas is where a pick is worth a second look - not a betting tip.

Picks lock when the first game of the season tips off. The lines are frozen, so everyone plays the same numbers.
